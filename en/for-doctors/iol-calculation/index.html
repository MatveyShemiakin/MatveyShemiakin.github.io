07Keratoconus

Use keratoconus-specific modes. In a network meta-analysis, Barrett True-K with measured posterior corneal curvature ranked highest overall. Kane Keratoconus is an option for cross-checking.

08Toric IOL

Compare Barrett Toric, Kane Toric and EVO Toric. Consider how posterior corneal astigmatism and effective lens position are accounted for. After LASIK or PRK, use the dedicated Barrett True-K Toric mode.

09After PK or DALK

Start by cross-checking keratometry against corneal topography or tomography. Graft irregularity can materially affect the refractive prediction. Published evidence is insufficient to favour one formula across all such eyes.
