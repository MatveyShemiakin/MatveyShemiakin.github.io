OHTS: ocular hypertension — treat or observe?

Individual risk of developing glaucoma and the OHTS–EGPS calculator.

2026-11-12 · English summary

OHTS enrolled 1,636 participants with ocular hypertension without established glaucomatous damage. At five years the probability of developing primary open-angle glaucoma was 9.5% with observation and 4.4% with topical treatment. Individual risk matters. The OHTS–EGPS model uses age, untreated intraocular pressure, central corneal thickness, vertical cup-to-disc ratio and visual field pattern standard deviation. It estimates risk of onset, not progression of established glaucoma.
