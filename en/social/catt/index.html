CATT: ranibizumab and bevacizumab for neovascular AMD

Comparative outcomes and treatment regimens.

2026-11-20 · English summary

CATT compared ranibizumab and bevacizumab for neovascular AMD. Visual acuity outcomes were comparable when the same dosing regimen was used. As-needed treatment involved monthly assessment and treatment when indicated. Comparable outcomes do not imply unrestricted interchangeability; ophthalmic bevacizumab use is off-label and local requirements must be considered.
